Our Story
❦

Once upon a time (aka fall 2022), two souls casually scrolling on Hinge got a notification — it's a match! Cory Anton Allen and Shornakay Kristina Wright started talking, both probably thinking there was a slim chance this was going anywhere. But with optimism, Shornakay mentioned it was the day after her birthday — and Cory used that opportunity to ask her out. She said yes. Their first date was set for Friday, November 18th, at a Brooklyn bowling alley, where sparks flew, laughs were shared, and neither of them wanted the night to end. What started as casual quickly turned into something neither could ignore.

From traveling across the globe to countless nights painting NYC red, Cory and Shornakay became inseparable. Two years later, they moved in together. Not long after, Cory decided it was time to level up — and because one proposal just wasn't enough, he did it twice. The first was a sweet, private moment just between them. The second? A surprise proposal during a double date at a packed Colombian restaurant. Why not make it unforgettable? Now, with love, laughter, and a lifetime of adventures ahead, Cory and Shornakay invite you to celebrate their forever on June 19th, 2027.

P.S. — Yes, some of you might be wondering… who is Shornakay? Shornakay is Krissie. She's Jamaican, where nicknames aren't optional — they're basically your government name. So if you hear Krissie, Shornakay, or any variation in between, just know they all refer to the same bride.
