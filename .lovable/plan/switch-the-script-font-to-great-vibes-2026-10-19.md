# Switch the script font to Great Vibes

Swap Parisienne for Great Vibes everywhere the site uses script lettering: the names, the envelope labels, section accents and the footer names.

## Steps
- Load Great Vibes in place of Parisienne in the font link.
- Change all 7 `font-family: 'Parisienne'` rules in `public/wedding.html` to `'Great Vibes', cursive`.
- Great Vibes looks a little smaller than Parisienne at the same size, so check the names and headings on phone and desktop. Adjust sizes only where text looks too small or wraps badly.
- Confirm there is no sideways scrolling on phones and that the envelope and footer still look balanced.

Nothing else on the site changes.
